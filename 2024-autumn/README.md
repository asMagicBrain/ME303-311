# 2024 Autumn course materials

Historical archive of the [original 2024 course page](https://me311.ancorasir.com/): 52 original files (49 PDF, 1 PNG, 1 XLSX, 1 ZIP). It includes the directly linked course teaching files, approved references and samples, and calendar available at archival preparation.

Do not submit work, join historical groups, register accounts, or provide personal information through old dates, submission links, forms or QR codes in these files. They are retained as historical source content, not current course instructions.

Files retain their original filenames and source bytes. Existing author, publisher, instructor and contributor credits remain in the originals. Redistribution of these listed references and student samples was confirmed by the course owner; this archive does not grant a blanket license to third-party content. Anonymous student examples may retain handwritten corrections and assessment marks.

External course services (including forms, account registration, CAD workspaces and Feishu where applicable) are not mirrored. General web images and vendor demonstration media are outside this file archive. This folder does not claim to archive every external dependency or publish a live course site.
